This is a review and detailed measurements of the Topping KB22 professional audio interface (ADC/DAC), headphone amplifier and keyboard. It was sent to me by the company and costs US $269.
Yes, this is an audio interface stuffed into a keyboard! I expected the keyboard to be cheap but the unit feels very substantial. The metal case gives it incredible heft, sharply increasing its chances of staying put once you dangle your cables from it. I am not into keyboard but this comes in two variations with different switch mechanism, replaceable switches, key mapping, etc. Sadly for me at least, it lacks direct playback controls (pause, play, skip, etc.). They are available as shifted function keys and such which make them not useful as I can by then use the mouse. Would have been great if on the top unused area we had such keys.
A very visible, though small, display lets you configure the modes and many operational aspects of the interface. You can however do that from its PC software. Audio interfaces can be overwhelming to use as just a DAC. Topping deals with this with a mode switch which highly simplifies the UI if you put it in "audiophile mode." Another mode for gaming exists which I could not figure out.
Software support inside the app is extensive and I am not qualified to characterize it. Suffice it to say, it seems to have a ton of effects/processing. Importantly we have parametric EQ.
Connectivity is nice with such features as OTG and USB hub:
On one side you have a power switch and on the other, 3.5mm and 1/4 headphone jacks.
I tested the unit with USB data and power over one cable. At first, I had a heck of a time getting more than 2 volts output of it. Then I realized the volume setting in Windows was defaulting to 67 every time I started a new test (opened the device). Same was happening on the input. This is through WASAPI interface. Maybe works better through ASIO, I don't. Either way, this shouldn't be happening. Sample rate was also limited to just 44.1 kHz in WASAPI. KB22 app has a drop down for other sample rates but the device itself needs to expose all rates. I am communicating with Topping on what is going on here.
I am going to run a subset of my tests since I can't run above 44.1 kHz anyway. Even if that was not the case, I would have had to triple the ticket prices given how much functionality there is here.
Topping KB22 DAC Measurements 
Using our standard dashboard, we see DAC performance that finishes at the top of the class for an audio interface:
Distortion is extremely low and below threshold of hearing at -120 dB. 
Dynamic range is superb for any DAC let alone an audio interface
We are talking 20+ bits here!
IMD test shows a hint of "ESS IMD Hump" in one channel:
It is a minor thing and as noted, this is excellent results for an interface.
Filter out of band attenuation is superb for a DAC:
Resulting in this excellent wideband THD+N response:
Yes, there is a slight rise at low frequencies but still, this is really, really good result.
Topping KB22 ADC Measurements 
Let's start with our dashboard here as well:
Here, I could not reach the stated spec of 24 dBu with the ADC clipping at slightly above 2.2 volt. Perhaps I have some setting wrong. Regardless, we land at nearly top of the class here:
Dynamic range is once again excellent for a DAC:
Computed EIN is a hair better than the spec at 150 ohm input loading.
IMD sweep delivers startlingly good results:
Albeit, level limited as noted.
Wideband noise+distortion is beyond excellent:
Topping KB22 Headphone Amplifier Measurements 
Budget audio interfaces tend to have dismal headphone outputs. Let's see how Topping does:
We have ample amount of power together with very low noise and distortion! You can drive all but the most demanding headphones with ease.
Conclusions 
Combination of audio interface and keyboard may be odd but saves a lot of desk space. At the retail price of KB22, you expect a cheap keyboard, audio interface, or both. You get the exact opposite: KB22 embarrasses most ADC and DACs in audio interfaces. While I am not qualified to review the keyboard, it is seems to run should to shoulder with premium specialty/gaming keyboards. 
Topping is clearly flexing its DAC muscles in audio interface world now. Its competitors better start to take them seriously. Very seriously!
I am happy to recommend the Topping KB22 keyboard and audio interface. It is a category busting device!
